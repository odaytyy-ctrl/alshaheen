Privacy Policy

ALSHAHEEN is a MetaTrader 5 analysis and trading terminal for Windows. It runs entirely on your own computer, and the developer receives none of your data.

01Who this policy is from

ALSHAHEEN (“we”, “us”) is published and operated by an individual developer trading under that name. This policy covers the ALSHAHEEN desktop application distributed through the Microsoft Store, and nothing else.

02What the app reads

ALSHAHEEN does not ask you for personal details, and it has no sign-up or login of its own. It reads information from the MetaTrader 5 terminal installed and signed in on the same computer, using MetaQuotes’ own Python interface. That information is:

  • your broker account number, the broker’s name, the trade server name and the account currency;
  • account balance, equity, margin, free margin and leverage;
  • your open positions — ticket, volume, prices, stop loss, take profit and floating profit — and the account’s recent deal history;
  • instrument prices, spreads, candle and tick history, and the broker’s list of instruments with their contract specifications.

MetaTrader 5 hands over the whole account record at once, and that record also contains the account holder’s name. ALSHAHEEN does not use that name and does not keep or write it anywhere.

To find the terminal, the app looks at the names of the programs running on your computer and at the program folders on your disk. If no terminal is running, it may start the one you installed. So that it can read their prices, it may add the instruments you use to MetaTrader 5’s Market Watch list.

All of it is read locally, repeatedly while the app runs — every few seconds — and used to draw the interface, to size and check the trades you place or arm, and to measure your exposure. Some of it is written into the files listed in section 03. None of it is transmitted to us.

03What the app writes, and where

ALSHAHEEN keeps working files on your own machine so it can show your history and resume after a restart. When installed from the Microsoft Store it uses two folders: a data folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\ALSHAHEEN, and a log folder inside it, %LOCALAPPDATA%\ALSHAHEEN\logs. Because ALSHAHEEN is a Microsoft Store app, Windows may keep these folders in the app’s own private storage instead (under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\), where they are removed together with the app.

These files never leave your computer. You can open, edit, back up or delete any of them. Advanced users can move both folders with the ALSHAHEEN_DATA and ALSHAHEEN_LOG_DIR environment variables.

04The one connection the app makes

Stated plainly

While the link to your broker is down, ALSHAHEEN checks every few seconds whether your own internet is working. It opens a plain TCP connection to 8.8.8.8:53 (Google Public DNS) and, only if that fails, to 1.1.1.1:53 (Cloudflare). No data is sent and no query is made — the connection is opened and closed. As with any internet connection, the operator of those addresses can see that your IP address contacted them.

That is the only outbound connection ALSHAHEEN itself makes. The subscription check in section 05 is carried out by Windows. The links to these terms and this policy open in your web browser, and the Subscribe button opens the Microsoft Store app, only when you click them. The connection to your broker is opened by the MetaTrader 5 terminal, not by ALSHAHEEN, and is governed by your broker’s and MetaQuotes’ own terms.

05Your subscription

ALSHAHEEN is sold as a monthly subscription through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the entire purchase. Through the standard Windows.Services.Store interface the app asks Windows whether this copy currently holds an active subscription and when it ends, and — if none is found — whether your Microsoft account has ever owned it. It asks once after each start and again when you press Check again.

We never see, receive or store your name, payment card, billing address or Microsoft account. Purchases and refunds are covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

A successful answer is cached locally in license_state.json, so the app keeps working for up to 30 days if the Store cannot be reached. That file holds the time of the last successful check, its result and the add-on’s Store ID — nothing about you.

06Third parties

  • Your broker. Orders you place through ALSHAHEEN are sent to your own broker by MetaTrader 5. Each order carries the app’s identifier (the magic number 20360000 and the comment “SHAHEEN_<timeframe>”), so your broker can see that it came from ALSHAHEEN. Their privacy policy applies to your account with them.
  • MetaQuotes. MetaTrader 5 is their software, installed and signed in by you.
  • Microsoft. Distribution, payment and subscription management.
  • Google and Cloudflare. Only the connection described in section 04, which shows them your IP address and nothing else.
  • GitHub. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub sees your IP address when you open them.

There are no other third parties. ALSHAHEEN contains no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party analytics.

07Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing on our side to retain, export or erase. Everything lives on your machine for as long as you keep it.

To remove it: uninstall ALSHAHEEN from Windows Settings. If Windows kept the app’s files in its private storage, they are removed with it; otherwise delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\ALSHAHEEN folder, which holds both folders. If you moved them with the environment variables in section 03, delete those folders too. Your broker and MetaQuotes keep their own records of your account and your orders, under their own policies.

08Your rights

Data-protection law gives you the right to access, correct, export and erase personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so those rights are satisfied by design rather than by request — every file the app writes is listed in section 03 and is on your own computer.

09Children

ALSHAHEEN is financial software for adults and is not directed at children. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and it collects nothing from anyone of any age.

10Security

ALSHAHEEN never sees and never stores your broker password — you sign in to MetaTrader 5 yourself. It does write your account number and trade server name into the files listed in section 03; treat those files as private. They are ordinary plain-text files, protected by your own Windows user account.

Privacy Policy

ALSHAHEEN is a MetaTrader 5 analysis and trading terminal for Windows. It runs entirely on your own computer, and the developer receives none of your data.

Effective 9 September 2026 Version 1.0.0.0 Publisher ODAI ABUREESHEH
Nothing is sent to us No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports, no user accounts.
Nothing is sold Your data is never sold, shared, rented or used for advertising.
Everything stays local Your trading records are written to your own disk and stay there.

01Who this policy is from

ALSHAHEEN is published by ODAI ABUREESHEH (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the ALSHAHEEN desktop application distributed through the Microsoft Store, and nothing else.

02What the app reads

ALSHAHEEN does not ask you for personal details, and it has no sign-up or login of its own. It reads information from the MetaTrader 5 terminal already installed and signed in on the same computer, using MetaQuotes’ own Python interface. That information is:

  • your broker account number, broker name and trade server name;
  • account balance, equity, margin and leverage;
  • your open positions and their stop loss and take profit levels;
  • instrument prices, spreads and candle history for the symbols you analyse.

All of it is read locally, on demand, and used only to draw the interface and to decide the trades you have armed. None of it is transmitted to us.

03What the app writes, and where

ALSHAHEEN keeps working files on your own machine so it can show your history and resume after a restart. When installed from the Microsoft Store these live under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ALSHAHEEN.

FileWhat it holds
alshaheen.logThe running log: what the program did, and every warning and error it raised.
trades.csvA journal of trades opened and closed, with prices, volumes and results.
auto_state.jsonWhich instruments you have armed for automatic trading.
spread_history.jsonSpread readings collected during the session, per instrument and per day.
heartbeat.jsonA liveness file so a watchdog can tell a working program from a wedged one.
powers​/<symbol>.jsonYour per-instrument settings, which you can edit yourself at any time.
These files never leave your computer. You can open, edit, back up or delete any of them.

04The one connection the app makes

Stated plainly

When ALSHAHEEN detects that the link to your broker has dropped, it opens a plain TCP connection to 8.8.8.8:53 (Google Public DNS) and 1.1.1.1:53 (Cloudflare) to find out whether your own internet is down or the broker’s server is unreachable. No data is sent and no query is made — the connection is opened and closed. As with any internet connection, the operator of those addresses can see that your IP address contacted them.

That is the only outbound connection ALSHAHEEN itself makes. The connection to your broker is opened by the MetaTrader 5 terminal, not by ALSHAHEEN, and is governed by your broker’s and MetaQuotes’ own terms.

05Your subscription

ALSHAHEEN is sold as a monthly subscription through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the entire purchase. The app asks Windows a single question — whether this copy currently holds an active subscription — through the standard Windows.Services.Store interface.

We never see, receive or store your name, payment card, billing address or Microsoft account. Purchases and refunds are covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

The answer Windows gives is cached locally so the app keeps working during a short outage. That cache records only the date of the last successful check.

06Third parties

  • Your broker. Orders you place through ALSHAHEEN are sent to your own broker by MetaTrader 5. Their privacy policy applies to your account with them.
  • MetaQuotes. MetaTrader 5 is their software, installed and signed in by you.
  • Microsoft. Distribution, payment and subscription management.

There are no other third parties. ALSHAHEEN contains no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party analytics.

07Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing on our side to retain, export or erase. Everything lives on your machine for as long as you keep it.

To remove it: uninstall ALSHAHEEN from Windows Settings, then delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\ALSHAHEEN folder. That is complete — no copy is held anywhere else.

08Your rights

Data-protection law gives you the right to access, correct, export and erase personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so those rights are satisfied by design rather than by request — you already have direct, unmediated access to every file the app writes.

09Children

ALSHAHEEN is financial software for adults and is not directed at children. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and it collects nothing from anyone of any age.

10Security

ALSHAHEEN stores no passwords and no broker credentials — it never sees them, because you sign in to MetaTrader 5 yourself. The working files described above are ordinary files protected by your own Windows user account.

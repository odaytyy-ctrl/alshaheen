source code of ALSHAHEEN, and no part of the application is published here.

## Before it will run

ALSHAHEEN does not work on its own. **MetaTrader 5 must already be installed on
the same PC and signed in to your own broker account**, and Algo Trading must be
enabled in it. The Microsoft Store does not install MetaTrader 5.

ALSHAHEEN never asks for your broker sign-in and never sees it. Orders go to
the account your own MetaTrader 5 terminal is signed in to, through MetaQuotes'
own interface.

## Risk

**Trading carries a risk of loss.** Positions are opened on margin, so a small
move against you can cost a large share of the money committed to that trade.
ALSHAHEEN is not a broker, not an adviser and not an investment service, and
nothing it shows you is investment advice. The Terms of Use set this out in
full; read them before you use it.
